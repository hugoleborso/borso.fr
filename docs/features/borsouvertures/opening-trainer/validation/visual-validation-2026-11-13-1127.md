## Notes

- **03, FAIL.** Deck rows carry neither the side nor the retention figure. The side appears only on the deck page ("You play Black"). Retention appears only on Today's Retention tab and on Stats. With Albin at 90%, its Decks row still read `91 cards · 0 due · 81 new`.
- **38, FAIL.** Explore is the one screen that breaks the fold rule.
  - The drill stacks the mode, "Change selection", moves and arrows controls, the board, the drill prompt, the action buttons and "Lines visited" in one 1268 px column. At 375 x 812 the board is cut by the tab bar and nothing under it is visible (`./visual-validation-2026-11-13-1127/17-explore-drill-375.png`).
  - The browse view nests a 360 px list scroller inside a page that scrolls, and its only action sits under the tab bar until the page scrolls.
  - DESIGN.md's "Applied today" list leaves out Explore. The brief asks for the rule on every screen.
- **40, FAIL.** Explore line rows are fixed at 64 px while their content needs 75 px (measured with `scrollHeight` on rows 3 to 5), so the ECO label overlaps the next row. This was seen in the Albin Countergambit variation, where names wrap to three lines.
- **19, UNVERIFIABLE.** Both devices' events for the 1. d4 card were kept and counted (31/38 on both). The retention half of the claim cannot be isolated, because the preview account already held 28 seeded events.
- **20, UNVERIFIABLE.** Removing a deck folder needs a new build. A browser cannot do it.
- **24, UNVERIFIABLE.** `content/traps.json` gives 10 traps to each of the five decks, so the "no matching puzzle" state does not exist in this build.
- **28, UNVERIFIABLE.** Which of `reviewedAt` and `receivedAt` the replay uses changes no visible output that could be told apart here.
- **07.** The spec puts the sign-in link in the top bar. The phone-first redesign moved it into the settings sheet, and `apps/borsouvertures/DESIGN.md` says "The top bar is 48 px and holds only the name and that button". The row was judged against the redesign.
- **23.** `set offline` and `network route --abort` do not reach requests a service worker makes. Under them the worker was fetched and cached anyway (cache `stockfish-19.0.1`). The row passed only once an init script blocked `navigator.serviceWorker.register`. It reads:

  ```js
  sw.register = () => Promise.reject(new Error('service worker disabled by validator'));
  ```

  This is a check of the app's handling of a failed load, not of a real offline phone.
- **26.** The "Sentry receives it" part leaves the browser and was not observed. The 400 came from an init script that wraps `fetch` for `POST /api/review-events`.
- **37.** A tap on a piece and then on a square does not move it, by mouse or by touch: `phone.sh touch` on `#bors-board-square-d7` got "no click with pointerType touch reached the target". Pieces move only by dragging. A touch drag over CDP works. The spec does not require tap-to-move, so the row passes.
- **33.** Deck line names stay in English under French ("Main line with 5. g3"). Deck names and ideas are translated.
- **05.** Accuracy is per deck, not per opening.
- **Observed, not a spec row.** Stats ("The share of due cards recalled on the first try…") and Explore ("Learn and play over the whole catalogue…") carry subtitles that explain the screen. `docs/standards/05-frontend-architecture.md` ("The interface does not explain itself") asks for none.
- **Preview data.** Sync tests pushed about 15 review events from this run to the preview account.
- No console errors were recorded on `vv1` or `vv2`. Every broken-image scan returned `[]`.
