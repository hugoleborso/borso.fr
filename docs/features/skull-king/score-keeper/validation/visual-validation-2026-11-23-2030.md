## Notes

- **09 (FAIL).** At a home-screen table of Hal, Bart and Carl, Carl has no stored avatar and sits in seat 3. He is drawn with Bart's exact pirate (bandana, eyepatch, cheek scar, pipe, parrot), and only the disc colour tells them apart. Game BXWW keeps the duplicate. Bart's stored record equals `suggestAvatar(1)`, and Carl is index 1 in `skull-king.regulars`. So a legacy regular takes the pirate of his position in the regulars list, not of his table seat. That lets him collide with a regular whose stored pirate is that seat's suggestion. In the server-seeded JYHZ game, Carl (seat 2) does not collide, so the defect is on the home-screen and new-game path. Evidence: `17-regulars-at-table.png`, `21-new-game-bart-carl.png`. On the bare home row, Carl and Bart also look identical (`06-home.png`).
- **10 (FAIL).** This has the same cause as row 09. A regular whose stored part is no longer offered (`hair: 'mohawk'`) falls back to `suggestAvatar(<regulars index>)` (6 for Hal) instead of the pirate of the seat he occupies (seat index 0). Evidence: `20-unknown-part-reads-as-seat.png`.
- **16 (UNVERIFIABLE).** The "same face on every phone" claim needs two devices or two browser engines. Within one browser, identical records render identical SVG markup on every screen.
- **Side effect of the run.** Game BXWW was created in the local dev database by row 09. In the session's local storage, the regulars list and Hal's avatar were restored to their seeded values. `skull-king.last-pirate` now holds Yan's edited pirate.

## Verdict: FAIL
