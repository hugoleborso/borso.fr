## Notes

- **Row 24 (FAIL).** At 375 px the bars list drops the OWNER and CONCERT MOOD columns entirely — the header row is only NAME / STATUS / FOLLOW-UP — and the List/Kanban toggle disappears too, so the kanban card (the other surface the spec says carries the owner and the mood) is unreachable on a phone. The only way to see who owns a bar, or its mood, on a 375 px screen is to open the bar in the form. Measured breakpoint: the two columns appear at ≥ 768 px and are absent at 375 px and 520 px. The spec's two rules — *"The list view shows the owner's first name in its own column"* and *"The list view shows the mood in its own column, sortable"* — carry no viewport qualifier, and the repo standard requires every page to render correctly at 375 px. This may well be an intentional responsive decision, in which case the fix is a sentence in the spec rather than code; either way it is a user-visible gap between what the spec asserts and what a phone shows, so it is not silently passed here.
- **Row 26 (UNVERIFIABLE).** No touch pass was run with `scripts/argent.sh`. Nothing in these four specs claims a hover-only or tap-specific affordance, so there was no assertion a synthetic click could falsely pass; the row is recorded as unverified rather than asserted, per the rule that a click is not a tap.
- **Row 27 (UNVERIFIABLE).** These specs make no claim about reduced motion or colour scheme, so there was nothing to check; recorded for completeness.
- **Test-harness notes (not findings about the feature).** The app was seeded through `POST /api/__test/seed?fixture=basic-band` and driven as the seeded admin `hugo`. Re-running that seed mid-run recreates the members and wipes the bars, which invalidates the browser session; the resulting failed logins then trip the API's 15-minute, 5-attempt login rate limiter, which the login screen reports as "Wrong name or password" rather than as a rate limit. The run was completed by setting an `x-forwarded-for` header on the browser so the limiter keyed a fresh bucket. Both are logged to the friction log.

## Verdict: FAIL
