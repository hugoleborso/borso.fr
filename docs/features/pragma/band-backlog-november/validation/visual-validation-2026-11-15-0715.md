## Notes

No FAIL and no UNVERIFIABLE rows. Here are the observations outside the spec's claims, for the reader to judge. None of them changed a verdict.

- **Done pushes a history entry.** It does not step back. Measured in a fresh browser. The path was practice `?view=songs`, then Order, then Done, which landed on `/sessions/<id>` without `?view=songs` and raised `history.length` from 4 to 5. Browser back then reopened `/setlists/<id>/order?back=…`. Behaviour 2 names only the back link, and behaviour 5 only says Done returns to the practice, so both rows pass. Still, a member who taps Done and then the phone's back button sees the Order screen again, and the Songs view they had chosen is not kept.
- **The bottom nav marks the wrong section on the Order screen.** Opened from a practice, it highlights "Setlists" rather than "Sessions" (`05-order-screen-practice.png`). The spec makes no claim about this.
- **The drag in row 11 used a mouse, not touch.** Behaviour 6 claims the order shows after Done, not that a touch drag works, so `scripts/phone.sh drag` was not needed for the verdict.
