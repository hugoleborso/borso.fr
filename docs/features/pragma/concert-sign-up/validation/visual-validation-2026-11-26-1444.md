## Notes

- **Row 08, UNVERIFIABLE.** The spec and the mockups show only whole hours in the "20 h" style. The implementation formats other hours two ways. A concert at 08:00 shows "08 h" with a leading zero (`33-…png`), and a concert at 09:30 shows "9:30" (`32-…png`). The 20:00 concert shows "20 h", as specified. The spec does not say how minutes or single-digit hours should look, so this row is left to the operator. In the mockups' style a reader would expect "8 h" and "9 h 30".
- **Row 10, FAIL.** On a phone the message is placed after the prize coupon, so it ends up under the coupon instead of under the venue. This holds with a poster (`09-poster-375-bottom.png`) and without one (`07-no-poster-with-prize-375.png`). The validated mockup `sans-affiche-375.png` shows venue, then message, then the dashed line and the coupon at the bottom of the ticket. At 1280 the order is right: the message is under the venue and the coupon is at the bottom (`10-…png`, `11-…png`).
- **Row 26, FAIL.** Here is how to reproduce it. First sign up for the next concert, A, so it shows "Inscrit·e". Then add an earlier concert, B, that this browser never signed up for, and reload `/`. The page shows B's ticket with "Inscrit·e / Un·e ami·e" (`33-stale-registered-state-on-new-concert.png`). It still did 5 s later. localStorage held only `pragma.registration.<A>` and `pragma.registration.<other>`, with no key for B. A second reload shows "Je serai là !". The same thing happened the first time a new next concert replaced Punk Paradise (row 01 run). So a fan who signed up for one concert can be shown as registered for the next one, and never gets a "Je serai là !" button on that first visit. The cause was not probed.
- **Row 31, UNVERIFIABLE.** The local dev server has no uploads bucket. The API log shows `UploadsConfigError: UPLOADS_BUCKET not set` at `presignPutObject`, so no poster can be stored here, and the replace and remove buttons never appear. Under `pnpm dev` the ERP reads the announcement through the batched `POST /api/read-batch`, so a routed `GET …/announcement` was never requested and could not fake a stored poster. A PR preview, which has the bucket, is needed to check replace, remove and the uploaded PNG end to end. The error alert for a failed save does render.
- **Row 32, observation.** In the trimmed PNG, column 0 is white on 54% of its height, while columns 1 and beyond are not. This is a one-pixel anti-aliased residue at the left edge, not a margin. At 375 px it is drawn at about 0.4 px.
- **Rows 05, 06 and 29** used a routed `upcoming` response whose `posterUrl` points at the PNG from row 32, served by Vite under `/@fs/…`. This was needed because of the bucket limit in row 31. Everything else in that payload is the real API answer.
- **Rows 15 and 16** wrapped `window.fetch` in the page to delay only the registration POST. Without the delay the opening screen lasts about 100 ms locally, too short for a screenshot.
- **Test data left in the dev database.** Three extra concerts (Le Trabendo, Le Café de la Danse, La Cigale) were moved to 20 Nov 2026. Punk Paradise was restored to 17 Dec 20:00 with its prize and message, and now holds the test registrations.

## Verdict: FAIL
