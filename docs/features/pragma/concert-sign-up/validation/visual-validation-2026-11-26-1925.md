## Notes

- **Row 08, FAIL.** The poster is sized as if the message were not there. With a poster, a prize and a message on a phone, the message is the last part of the ticket, and it ends 43 px under the top of the fixed "Je serai là !" bar. At 375 × 812, the ticket ends at y 755, the bar starts at 712 and the page is 867 px tall. The message shows half hidden behind the button (`01-home-poster-375.png`) and only shows in full after a scroll (`02-home-poster-375-scrolled.png`). The overlap is 43 px at 375 × 667, 390 × 844 and 360 × 740 too. Without a message the poster keeps the exact same height (y 62–481), and the ticket then ends at y 691, above the bar (`19-poster-no-message-tshirt-375.png`). This is measured. That the fit leaves out the message height is inferred from those two measurements, not read in the code. The seeded concert has a message, so a fan sees this case first.
- **Row 32, UNVERIFIABLE.** The local API presigns the upload with the `AI-Dev-ReadOnly` key. A `curl` PUT to a fresh presigned URL answered 403 `AccessDenied ... s3:PutObject ... explicit deny`, and the browser's CORS preflight answered 403. No poster can be stored from this sandbox, so replacing a poster and seeing the uploaded PNG on `/` needs a PR preview. The client side up to the PUT works. It accepts the PDF, draws and trims it (row 33), and requests `poster-upload` (200). One observation for that preview check, not probed: the presigned PUT URL carries `x-amz-checksum-crc32=AAAAAA%3D%3D`, which is the CRC32 of an empty body, while the PUT sends 1.3 MB.
- **Observation, no row.** The bottom sheets hold a react-aria hidden dismiss button whose accessible name is "Dismiss" in English, also with the French locale. It is 1 × 1 px and not visible, so it does not break the visible-string rule, but a French screen reader reads it in English.
- **Observation, no row.** Earlier today's report flagged "08 h" for a concert at 08:00. This run shows "8 h" (`29-today-earlier-concert-shown.png`).
- **Rows 15 and 16** wrapped `window.fetch` in the page to delay only the registration POST. Without the delay the opening screen lasts about 100 ms locally, too short to shoot.
- **Dev database state left behind.** The seeded poster was removed (row 31) and could not be put back, because `PATCH …/announcement` refuses the seeded key with 422 `foreign-poster-key` and the upload is blocked (row 32). `POST /api/__test/seed` restores it. The concert keeps its 3 Dec 20:29 date, its prize "3 bières à gagner" (beer) and its message. It now holds the test registrations Camille, Jo, Nina, Paul and Lou. The three concerts created for rows 01, 26 and 35 were deleted.

## Verdict: FAIL
