## Notes

- **Row 10 — six songs at first paint.** At 375 × 812 the list starts at `y = 427` (breadcrumb, title, four action buttons, the member tab row, the energy sparkline) and the fixed "Copy order / Add song" bar cuts in at `y = 695`, so a member opening the setlist sees **3 songs fully and 3 partly** before touching the screen; six become visible after ~430 px of scrolling. The density work itself is sound — the 82 px pitch is real (rows 01–05) and the spec's own input metric, scoped to *the list region*, passes as row 06. What the card gained, the page chrome above it spends. Treat this row as a product call rather than a defect in the column if the "list region" reading was the intended one.
- **Row 16 / row 33 — the column silently disappears between 640 px and 780 px.** With a title long enough to take the row (an 80-character title injected through the API for the test), the inline column at `sm`+ is squeezed to a measured width of **0 px**. `slotsFittingWidth` treats `availableWidthPx <= 0` as "not measured yet" and returns every slot, so four slots render inside an `overflow: hidden` box of zero width and **no `+N` appears** — exactly the failure the decision row "Where does the slot budget come from?" says the measured budget eliminated. Screenshot `16-700-column-clipped-to-zero.png` shows row 03 with no column and no marker while the other five rows show four icons. The marker does work where the column keeps a sliver: `+2` at 320 px, `+4` at 840–880 px.
- **Row 21 — more primary instruments than slots costs every icon.** With 7 primary instruments against a budget of 6 (4 members + 2), the cap itself is computed correctly, but the column latches at a 16 px measured width on first paint and renders **only `+7`** on every row, at 1280 px, with 726 px of free title space beside it. It does not recover on reload, on a fresh daemon, or after seconds of settling; at exactly 6 primaries the column renders all six slots at 114 px, and at 5 it renders five at 95 px. So the regression appears precisely when the overflow counter is owed from the first render — the reader of a band with more instruments than slots sees no instrument at all.
- **Row 20 — reachable through the API only.** `formValuesToLineup` turns an all-empty selection into "no override", so saving an emptied lineup in the editor clears the override instead of storing an empty one; the grey-beats-yellow precedence was therefore exercised with a direct `PUT` of an all-empty override. The precedence itself is correct.
- **Row 15 — touch drag not exercised.** The energy gesture was driven with real pointer events plus a reload, which is what the spec's test strategy asks for, and a real touch *tap* (argent) was checked not to commit a value. A real touch *drag* was not sent: `scripts/argent.sh` notes `gesture-swipe` is unsupported on Chromium and `gesture-drag` dispatches mouse input, so it would prove nothing a pointer drive did not.
- **Row 22 / row 29 — the seeded database ships every instrument at `icon = 'music'`.** The migration seeds `position` but leaves `icon` at its default, so out of the box all five slots render the same fallback glyph and the column separates players by colour alone (`./visual-validation-2026-09-20-2049/25-375-fallback-glyphs-restored.png`). That matches the spec's edge case and the ADR (the instruments page is where icons are picked), but "who plays what" only becomes readable once somebody opens that page.
- **Measured figures that differ slightly from the spec's prose.** The energy control measures 78 × 44 px, not "82 px"; a lineup slot is 19 px wide around a 17 px glyph, not "20 px". Neither changes the behaviour the decisions rest on (32 px per level, ten levels, a 44 px target, a column that survives at 360 px).
