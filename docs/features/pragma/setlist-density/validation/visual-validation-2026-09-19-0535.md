## Notes

- **Row 05 and row 07 and row 11 — the six-song metric.** At 375×667 the list sits in a band 479 px tall, between a `sticky top-0 z-20` energy chart that ends at y=71 and a full-width `bg-bg/95 backdrop-blur` action bar that starts at y=550, with the tab bar below it. Six cards plus their seams span 528 px, so no scroll position shows six of them; a scan of every scroll position in 2 px steps returned a maximum of 5. The same scan returns 6 at 375×812 and 6 at 360×780. The spec states the width but never the height, and its own "before" figure of 2.5 songs per screen is 667 ÷ 270, so the 667 px height is the one the spec's arithmetic was done on. On that screen the feature delivers 5, not the 8.5 the Result table claims nor the 6 the input metric requires. The layout has to give back about 50 px of chrome, or the metric has to name a height.
- **Row 08 and row 20 — the title truncates at 360 px.** With a 20-character title the title box is 104 px wide and the text needs 131 px, so it clips to `Twenty Chars E…`. The column had already yielded to four slots plus `+1` and stopped there, holding 96 px, which is 92 px more than the title needed. The rule in the spec is that the column yields before the title and that the title never truncates to feed the column; the implementation gives the column a fixed budget instead of letting the title claim what it needs first. Re-verified in a fresh daemon after the wrapper's wedge warning.
- **Row 28 — a rejected energy write is silent.** With `PUT /api/setlists/*/entries/*` aborted, a drag from 9 to 7 left the row reading 7 for as long as it was watched, the page showed no failure line anywhere, and `GET /entries` still returned 9. A reload put 9 back. The failure line the spec points at exists — `SetlistEditor.tsx` threads a `failureKey` into `SetlistToolbar` and other mutations call `failWith(...)` — so the energy mutation is simply not wired to it. The user is shown a value the server refused, with nothing to tell them.
- **Row 37 — the column is empty on day one.** Straight after the migration and the seed, `GET /api/instruments` returned every instrument with `icon: "music"`, `position` 0/1/1/1/2, and not one player with `isPrimary: true`. Because slots are drawn from primary instruments only, every lineup column on every row rendered zero children and measured 4 px wide, while the member avatars the card used to carry are gone. The column only came to life after marking a primacy by hand on `/instruments`. The migration seeds `position` but nothing seeds `is_primary`, so every existing band loses the who-plays-what signal on the setlist until someone finds the instruments page. The spec's happy path 3 and 4 describe a column that, on the data as migrated, is not there.
- **Row 34, secondary observation, not counted as a failure.** The spec says existing rows seed `position` from family order then name. The three harmonic instruments all came out at position 1, so the tie is broken somewhere other than the stored position. The rendered order was stable across all six rows and across reloads, so the visible claim holds; the stored data does not match the sentence that describes it.
- **Observation, not counted.** The mobile lineup button measured 0.030 of a 673 px screen, about 20 px tall, below the 44 px target the energy meter was given. The spec sets no size for it.
- **Observation, not counted.** On the argent browser the album artwork fell back to the initials placeholder because that Chromium cannot reach the Deezer CDN. The `onError` fallback behaved as designed and the broken-image scan stayed empty.
- **Tooling.** Rows 17 and 18 were driven with real touch through `scripts/argent.sh`, on its own 500×673 Chromium, which is the width that browser gives and not one the spec names. `argent gesture-drag` sends a mouse drag rather than a finger slide, so the pure-touch slide was checked indirectly: the meter carries `touch-action: none`, so a finger slide on it cannot be taken by the page scroll. Every other row was driven through `scripts/browser.sh`. One agent-browser session wedged with CDP timeouts on `Page.navigate` mid-run and was replaced; both failing measurements were re-taken in a fresh daemon afterwards.

## Verdict: FAIL
