## Notes

- **01, the harness crashes on task 7 intermittently.** Over eight task 7 runs it crashed twice, both times while other browsers were driving another instance on the machine (my agent-browser sweep during run 1, and a deliberate sweep during the load reruns). The crash is `MAIN_TEXT_INCLUDES` or `LISTS_A_DURATION` in `page-probes.setup.ts` calling `document.querySelector('main').innerText` when the page has no `<main>` yet, in the walk `recordTakeAndTalk` (`task-walks.setup.ts`), after Send or after `driver.open` of the past practice. The counts never differed from the baseline when the walk finished. The spec asks for a harness that reproduces the baseline on a rerun, and a full `--compare` that exits 1 under ordinary machine load does not do that reliably; the walk needs to wait for `main` before reading it. Round 3 failed this row on a similar intermittent task 9 failure, so it is graded the same way here.
- **15, overlapping hit areas on the practice session's recordings list.** The harness counts a control as 44 px when its own box plus its `::after` insets reach 44, and does not check whether a neighbour's hit area covers part of it. On `/sessions/4523ac81-…` (practice) the 17 record buttons sit 41 px apart, so every one but the last has 41 px of height that is actually its own; the argent taps above show a tap inside the first button's declared area opening the second song's recording sheet. The other small controls checked (setlist drag handles, transition lines, `/instruments` handles and toggles, Edit, Detach, Delete on `/improvements`) pass the same test. Either the rows get at least 8 px between the buttons, or the button takes the 44 px box itself.
- **21, not graded as breakage, same reading as r4.** At 1280x812 on arrival on `/tasks`, the fixed "Save" pill (box 1147-1244 x 736-780) covers the lower right corner of the empty Notes field in the New task panel (921-1227 x 675-763); scrolled to the end, the two are clear (Notes ends at 361). That is the fixed strip passing over content mid-page, which r4 judged deliberate and the spec's bottom-padding rule allows. See [`21-tasks-1280-light-viewport.png`](./visual-validation-slice-1-r5/21-tasks-1280-light-viewport.png).
- **02** is fixed: the chevron is back on every task card and the harness counts 0 text-only buttons over 414.
- Rows 13 and 14 rest on the harness numbers; their r3 argent checks were not repeated. Slice 1 rows the spec gives to later slices are not graded.
- All servers and browsers started here (instances 5182 and 5190, argent, agent-browser) were stopped. The shared local Postgres cluster was already running and was left running.
