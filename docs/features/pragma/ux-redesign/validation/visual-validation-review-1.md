## Notes

- **R3-c FAIL: the subscription the operator asked for is not there.** The operator asked to subscribe to the band's calendar: a `webcal://` link on iOS that opens the subscribe dialog, a `calendar.google.com/calendar/r?cid=` link on Android, and a copy button. What shipped goes the other way: the member pastes their own calendar's address into the app, with steps for each platform. That part works (R3-a and R3-b pass), but nothing gives the member the band's sessions in their phone calendar. The spec's *Operator review 1* section says this was deliberately left out, because it needs a new public iCal route behind a per-member secret and an ADR first. Whether that exclusion is acceptable for this review is for the operator to decide. Seen from the operator's request, it is not delivered.
- R1 observation, not a failure: the snackbar clips its message at both widths ("“Lightning” removed from the setl…" at 375, "…removed from the s…" at 1280, where the bar is about 450 px wide on a 1 000 px content area). The song title and Undo stay readable.
- R1 test side effect: on the first try my Undo came after the six seconds, so Lightning stayed removed from Set principal (20 to 19 songs). One entry of the Festival setlist was also removed on purpose to time the snackbar. Both are in the throwaway database.
- R4 side effect: opening a round (to see results on the vote page) created the "Audience choice" setlist on the concert, which then shows on the concert page. Nothing about the vote shows there.
- R5-b limit: headless Chromium has no on-screen keyboard. I stood one in by shrinking the viewport by 336 px after typing. That models Android, where the page resizes; on iOS the keyboard covers the page instead, and that case is not reproduced. The reveal listener in `keyboard-reveal.adapter.ts` fires once, on the first `visualViewport` resize after focus. `scripts/argent.sh touch` resizes the viewport three times on its own, which uses up that listener, so the keyboard measurement was taken in a single CDP session without it. With it, the result read "2 results, title at y=142". That reading came from the test setup, not the app.
- R5-e: Add an instrument does not keep a draft after a backdrop tap or a swipe. That matches how this sheet behaved before ("drafts survive as today"). New task keeps its draft through a swipe.
- Members page, outside R7: the New member form is still an inline card above the matrix, and its Save is that page's one primary. The operator's review did not ask about it.

## Verdict: FAIL

R1, R2, R4, R5, R6, R7 and R8 pass at 375 and 1280, in light and dark, with real touch where touch matters. R3 fails only on its subscription half (R3-c), which the spec records as left out pending an ADR.
