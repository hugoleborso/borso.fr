## Notes

- **Dev server restart.** The server on :5174 was stopped by a time limit and restarted mid-run, which reseeded the database. The first undo loop failed with `TypeError: Failed to fetch` and then missing elements. That was the outage, not the app. I closed both browser sessions, signed in again and re-ran row 25 from scratch. Rows 01 to 06 ran before the restart against the same seeded state, which `/api/streaks` confirmed after it (Hugo 4 open, Léa 1 counted, band 1).
- **14, 19, 22, 27, 30, 33, 34, UNVERIFIABLE.** These cover the 20:00 job, Sentry tagging, the dispatch claim and the preview deployment. A browser can observe none of them. The spec routes them to the `streak-reminder` unit tests, the controller test and the CDK test.
- **29, UNVERIFIABLE.** The temporary member had no credentials (sign-in as `zoe` answered "Wrong name or password"), so she could not log a study. Her removal could not change the band count. The rate-limit bucket was reset after that failed sign-in. Proving this half needs a member with activity to be deleted, which would mean deleting a seeded member.
- **32, UNVERIFIABLE.** With `/api/streaks` aborted, the console showed nothing (measured). The client sends the failure to `Sentry.captureException` through the query's `reportFailure` meta (read in `streaks.queries.ts` and `error-reporting.adapter.ts`). Sentry delivery is not observable from the dev browser.
- **36, UNVERIFIABLE.** No take was recorded, so the claim that takes do not count was not exercised.
- **20.** The flame glyph is `ink-faint` at 0. The digit "0" is `ink` rgb(29, 25, 20), the same as the digit in every other state (measured). The Result section ties the tone to the flame, so I read "reads 0 in `ink-faint`" as the glyph.
- **01.** The band chip shows the `members` glyph and the count, with no flame glyph, as in the Result mockup `[👥 4]`.
- **02.** The `flame` path lives in `InstrumentGlyphs.tsx`, from an earlier commit (`7f7c1aab`), and `Icon.tsx` spreads it in. The visible glyph is present. Where it is declared is an engineering detail.
- Observed but not asserted by the spec. At 320 px the chips wrap below the title (h1 y 16–50, chips y 58–102), in `./visual-validation-2026-10-29-1350/22-home-320.png`. They sit on one line at 375 px. The page title is "Next up", not "Home", which predates this branch.
- Every screenshot ran the broken-image scan, and all returned `[]`.
