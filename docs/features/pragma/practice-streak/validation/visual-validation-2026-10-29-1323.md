## Notes

- **06, FAIL.** The flames inside the sheet's member rows do not use the accent family. Each row draws the flame in `text-on-color-dark` rgb(10, 9, 7) or `text-on-color-light` rgb(255, 253, 248) on a pill filled with the member's colour. They also have no state tone. Hugo's row (today open) and Marc's row (0) look the same as Léa's (counted), whereas the chips use `accent`, `accent-soft` and `ink-faint`. The spec states "The flame uses only the accent family from `tokens.css`", and its test strategy says this is "checked against computed styles". The spec also describes "a dot in the member's stored colour … and their flame" as two separate things. The implementation merges them into one coloured pill that carries the flame. The chips, the large flame and the band glyph do pass (rows 03–05).
- **25, FAIL (intermittent, 1 of 5 cycles).** After the first undo, Home showed "Your flame, 5 days, today counted" for at least 3 s and across a re-snapshot. A fresh `fetch('/api/streaks')` answered `{"days":4,"isTodayCounted":false}` at the same moment. Only a later navigation away and back corrected the chip. Cycles 2 to 5 were correct. The likely cause is inferred, not measured. A song with two parts makes one Practised tap send two `POST /api/studies` and one undo send two `DELETE`s, as the network log shows. Each response settles the `streaks` cache, so a response computed while one study still stood can land last and leave today counted.
- **14, 19, 22, 27, 29, 32, 33, UNVERIFIABLE.** These are the 20:00 job, Sentry tagging, the dispatch claim and the preview deployment. None is observable from a browser. The spec routes them to the `streak-reminder` unit tests, the controller test and the CDK test.
- **31, UNVERIFIABLE.** With `/api/streaks` aborted, the browser logged nothing (measured). `useStreaks` has no error reporting, and the shared `QueryClient` has no `onError`. The spec does not say which side "logs the error". A server-side log needs a server failure, which the browser cannot cause. Read on the client, this row would be a FAIL.
- **35, UNVERIFIABLE.** No take was recorded, so the claim that takes do not count was not exercised.
- Observed but not asserted by the spec: at 320 px the chips wrap below the title (h1 at y 16–50, chips at y 58–102), visible in `./visual-validation-2026-10-29-1323/16-home-320.png`. They sit on one line at 375 px, the width the spec and the standards require. The page title is "Next up", not "Home". This predates the branch: the diff of `HomePage.tsx` only adds lines, and `home.title` is "Next up" in `en.json`.
- Every screenshot ran the broken-image scan, and all returned `[]`.

## Verdict: FAIL
