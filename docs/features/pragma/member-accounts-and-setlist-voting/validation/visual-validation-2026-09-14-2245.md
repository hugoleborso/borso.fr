## Notes

- **Row 19 (passkeys) — UNVERIFIABLE.** `/account` renders the passkey section, the "Name this device" field and the "Add a passkey" button, but the button is `disabled` in this headless Chromium because no platform authenticator is available. No passkey could therefore be created, named, removed, or used to sign in, and "a member with one can still sign in by password" has no subject. Needs a CDP virtual authenticator or a real device.
- **Row 34 (touch) — UNVERIFIABLE**, per the spec's own test-strategy section: `scripts/argent.sh gesture-swipe` returns success and moves nothing on Chromium, so every deck and proposal-row gesture here was a mouse-backed `gesture-drag` (plus one held `mouse down`/`move` sequence for the zone fill). That proves the Pointer Events mapping, the zone-to-points table and the budget refusal; it does not prove the deck behaves under a real thumb.
- **Observation, not a spec row — the target field does not reflect the stored target.** After applying a target of 2 (`GET /api/setlists/:id` returns `targetSongCount: 2`), a fresh load of the vote page renders `#vote-target` with the value `15` while the budget bar correctly reads `/ 6`. Visible in `14-budget-exhausted-refusal.png` and `17-close-panel-drag-reorder.png`. A member reading the field is told the wrong target.
- **Observation, not a spec row — the budget bar can show a negative remainder.** Lowering the target below what a member has already spent leaves the bar at `-1 / 6` (`15-new-song-mid-vote.png`). The spec never describes lowering a target mid-vote, so this is outside every assertion above, but it is a state a member can reach from the UI.
- The budget-exhausted copy was observed in English ("No points left! Take points off another song to keep liking."); the French string the spec quotes verbatim is present in `apps/pragma/site/src/i18n/fr.json:380`.

## Verdict: PASS_EXCEPT_UNVERIFIABLE
